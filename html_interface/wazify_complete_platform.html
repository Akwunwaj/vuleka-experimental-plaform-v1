❓ Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between Wazify's fee and Meta's fee?
▾
Wazify charges a flat monthly platform fee (R299–R1,999) for access to the dashboard, features, and support. Meta charges separately per WhatsApp conversation — typically R0.01–R0.05 per conversation. You pay Meta directly through your own Meta Business account. We never mark up Meta's fees.
Do my customers need to opt in before I can message them?
▾
Yes — this is both a Meta requirement and a POPIA requirement. Customers must actively opt in before you can send them marketing messages. Wazify provides QR codes, website widgets, and keyword opt-in flows to make this easy. Service messages (replies within 24 hours of a customer messaging you) are free and don't require a template.
What is a WhatsApp template and how do I get one approved?
▾
A WhatsApp template is a pre-approved message format required for sending outbound messages outside a 24-hour customer-initiated window. You submit templates through Meta's WhatsApp Business Manager. Wazify helps you submit templates directly from the Templates page. Approval typically takes 24–48 hours.
Can I use Wazify for multiple businesses?
▾
Yes — the Pro plan includes the Agency Portal which lets you manage unlimited client sub-accounts from one dashboard. Each client has their own contacts, campaigns, inbox, and billing. You can also white-label the entire platform under your own brand and domain and resell it as your own product.
What SA payment methods can I use to pay for Wazify?
▾
You can pay your Wazify subscription via PayFast (credit/debit cards, Instant EFT, SnapScan, Zapper), Ozow (Instant EFT from all major SA banks), PayGate, or Yoco payment links. PayFast is the default and supports auto-recurring monthly billing with tokenized card storage.
Is Wazify POPIA compliant?
▾
Yes. Wazify includes a full POPIA Compliance Centre with consent audit logs, double opt-in support, automatic opt-out on STOP keyword, data deletion on request, data retention policy settings, and downloadable compliance reports. Your compliance score is shown on the POPIA page.
